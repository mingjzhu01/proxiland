Proxiland Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Proxiland ("the App") is a professional networking app that helps you discover and connect with professionals physically near you. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email, and password, used to create and secure your account.
  • Profile information: anything you choose to add to your profile — headline, employer, job title, education, bio, and profile photo.
  • Location: your device's location, but only while you have explicitly turned on "visibility" in the app. Location is not collected in the background, and is not collected at all unless you actively enable visibility. Visibility automatically expires after the time window you choose.
  • Connection activity: requests you send or receive, and connections you make with other users.

How we use this information

  • To show you other users who are physically nearby and currently visible, and to show you to them, while your visibility is turned on.
  • To let you send and receive connection and coffee-meetup requests.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App.

What other users can see

Other users can see your profile information and your approximate distance from them only while your visibility is turned on. Your exact location is never shared with other users or visible to them — only distance.

Blocking and reporting

You can block another user at any time, which removes any existing connection with them, cancels pending requests between you, and prevents either of you from contacting the other going forward. You can also report a user; reports are reviewed by us.

Data retention and deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the information below.

Third-party services

We use Supabase to store account, profile, and location data, and to operate authentication. We do not sell your data to third parties.
